Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

HAJR Pulse ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by the HAJR Pulse channel owner. It is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Threads and YouTube through their official APIs.

Data we access

Only the information needed to publish: OAuth access tokens and basic profile info (account ID/username) of the owner's own accounts, as granted by the owner.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are never sold, shared or sent to any third-party server.

No third-party data

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other users, viewers or third parties, and uses no analytics, ads or tracking cookies.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's settings; local tokens are then deleted.
